# 🎯 HOW TO SEE THE "TAKE CLINICAL NOTES" BUTTON

## ⚠️ IMPORTANT: The Button Only Appears AFTER a Search!

**Looking at your screenshot:**

- ✅ You're on the Evidence Search page
- ❌ You haven't performed a search yet
- ❌ That's why the button isn't visible!

---

## 📝 STEP-BY-STEP: How to See the Button

### Step 1: Perform a Search

1. **Click in the search box** that says "Ask a clinical question..."
2. **Type a query**, for example:

   ```
   STEMI guidelines 2024
   ```

   OR click one of the suggested searches:

   - 🩺 DKA Management
   - 💊 Vancomycin Dosing
   - 📋 STEMI Guidelines
   - ⚠️ Drug Contraindications
   - 🔄 Drug Interactions

3. **Press Enter** or click the Search button (🔍)

### Step 2: Wait for Results

- You'll see a loading spinner ⏳
- Wait 10-30 seconds for AI to synthesize evidence
- Results will appear with summary and sources

### Step 3: Look for the Button

**AFTER the search completes, you'll see:**

```
┌───────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  [Search Results Title]                       │
│  [Summary of evidence...]                     │
│                                               │
│  [📝 Take Clinical Notes] ← APPEARS HERE!    │
│                                               │
│  [Detailed results continue below...]        │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

The button appears as a **blue button** above the results, on the right side!

## 🤔 Why Is It Designed This Way?

**Intentional UX Decision:**

- **Before search:** No button (nothing to take notes about yet)
- **After search:** Button appears (now you have evidence to reference!)
- **Purpose:** Button tied to specific search context

**Benefits:**

- Notes are linked to the search query
- Evidence summary is captured automatically
- Context is preserved (what were you researching?)
