## 🎨 Color Coding

### Recommendation Strength Badge:

| Badge | Color | Meaning |
|-------|-------|---------|
| **STRONG** | 🟢 Green | High confidence, do it |
| **WEAK** | 🟡 Yellow | Consider it, case-by-case |
| **CONDITIONAL** | 🔵 Blue | Patient-specific decision |
| **INSUFFICIENT** | ⚫ Gray | Can't recommend |

### Evidence Quality:

| Quality | Requirements |
|---------|--------------|
| **High** | Multiple RCTs, meta-analyses |
| **Moderate** | Some RCTs, good cohort studies |
| **Low** | Observational studies |
| **Very Low** | Case reports, expert opinion |

## 📈 Quality Indicators

### Look for These Signs of High Quality:

**Strong Evidence:**
- ✅ Multiple RCTs
- ✅ Meta-analyses included
- ✅ High consensus (>80%)
- ✅ Large sample sizes
- ✅ Recent studies (<5 years)

**Weak Evidence:**
- ⚠️ Observational studies only
- ⚠️ Low consensus (<60%)
- ⚠️ Small sample sizes
- ⚠️ Old studies (>10 years)
- ⚠️ High risk of bias

## 🎓 Learn More

**Understanding Recommendation Strength:**
- **Strong:** Benefits clearly outweigh harms (or vice versa)
- **Weak:** Benefits and harms closely balanced
- **Conditional:** Varies by patient/situation
- **Insufficient:** Can't determine balance

**Understanding Evidence Quality:**
- **High:** Very confident in effect estimate
- **Moderate:** Moderately confident
- **Low:** Limited confidence
- **Very Low:** Very uncertain

## 💡 Pro Tips

### Get the Best Results:
1. **Be specific** - "early antibiotics sepsis" not just "sepsis"
2. **Use clinical terms** - "tPA stroke" not "clot buster brain"
3. **Include population** - "steroids COPD" not just "steroids"
4. **Check limitations** - Important for edge cases

### Red Flags:
🚩 "Insufficient Evidence" = Don't use without other sources  
🚩 Low confidence (<50%) = High uncertainty  
🚩 No RCTs = Weaker evidence base  
🚩 Major limitations = Apply cautiously
