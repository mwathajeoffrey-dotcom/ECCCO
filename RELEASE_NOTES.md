## 📚 8 New Medical Comorbidity Topics

All new questions feature the **most current 2024-2025 clinical guidelines** and landmark trial data:

### 1️⃣ Cardiac Disease in Pregnancy (30 questions)
- Valvular disease management
- Congenital heart disease (WHO classification)
- Peripartum cardiomyopathy
- Mechanical valve anticoagulation
- **Guidelines:** ESC 2023/2024, ACC/AHA 2024

### 2️⃣ Diabetes in Pregnancy (30 questions)
- GDM screening and diagnosis
- Glucose targets and HbA1c goals
- Insulin and medication management
- Delivery timing strategies
- **Guidelines:** ADA 2025 Standards of Care

### 3️⃣ Hypertensive Disorders in Pregnancy (30 questions)
- **CHAP Trial 2022** evidence (practice-changing!)
- Chronic hypertension management
- Superimposed preeclampsia
- Medication protocols
- **Guidelines:** AHA/ACC 2024, ISSHP 2024

### 4️⃣ Thromboembolism in Pregnancy (30 questions)
- VTE prevention and treatment
- LMWH dosing strategies
- Antiphospholipid syndrome (revised 2023 criteria)
- **ASRA 2024** neuraxial timing guidelines
- **Guidelines:** ASH 2024, ACCP 2021

### 5️⃣ Infectious Disease in Pregnancy (30 questions)
- HIV management (U=U concept)
- Hepatitis B and C protocols
- GBS prophylaxis (CDC 2024 updates)
- TORCH infections
- COVID-19 and vaccination
- **Guidelines:** CDC 2024, NIH 2024, WHO 2024

### 6️⃣ Renal Disease in Pregnancy (30 questions)
- CKD staging and outcomes
- Dialysis intensification strategies
- Renal transplant management
- Preeclampsia vs CKD flare differentiation
- **Guidelines:** KDIGO 2024, ASN 2024

### 7️⃣ Thyroid Disorders in Pregnancy (30 questions)
- Pregnancy-specific TSH targets
- Hypothyroidism and hyperthyroidism
- Antithyroid drug protocols
- TRAb monitoring
- Postpartum thyroiditis
- **Guidelines:** ATA 2024, Endocrine Society 2024

### 8️⃣ Hematologic Disorders in Pregnancy (30 questions)
- Anemia diagnosis and treatment
- Thrombocytopenia (gestational vs ITP)
- Sickle cell disease management
- Von Willebrand disease
- Transfusion medicine
- **Guidelines:** ASH 2024, NHLBI 2024
