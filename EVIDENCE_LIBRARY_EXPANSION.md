## 🏆 LANDMARK TRIALS ADDED BY CATEGORY

### 1. CARDIAC ARREST & RESUSCITATION (3 trials)
✅ **ROC ALPS Trial (NEJM 2016)** - Amiodarone vs Lidocaine
- DOI: 10.1056/NEJMoa1514204
- Impact: Established amiodarone as preferred antiarrhythmic (24.4% vs 23.7% survival)
- NNT: Not statistically significant but trend favoring amiodarone

✅ **ETCO₂ Monitoring Systematic Review (Resuscitation 2020)**
- DOI: 10.1016/j.resuscitation.2020.03.004
- Impact: ETCO₂ as best CPR quality indicator
- Key: <10 mmHg at 20 min → 94% poor outcome

✅ **TTM2 Trial (NEJM 2021)** - Already included, kept
- DOI: 10.1056/NEJMoa2100591
- Impact: Normothermia non-inferior to hypothermia

### 2. SEPSIS & SEPTIC SHOCK (4 trials)
✅ **CLOVERS Trial (NEJM 2023)** - Restrictive vs Liberal Fluids
- DOI: 10.1056/NEJMoa2202707
- Impact: Restrictive fluid strategy non-inferior (14% vs 14.9% mortality)
- Changed practice toward judicious fluid use

✅ **ProCESS Trial (NEJM 2014)** - Early Goal-Directed Therapy
- DOI: 10.1056/NEJMoa1401602
- Impact: Ended era of rigid EGDT protocols (no benefit vs usual care)
- Mortality: 21.8% EGDT vs 18.9% usual care (p=0.83)

✅ **APROCCHSS Trial (NEJM 2018)** - Steroids in Septic Shock
- DOI: 10.1056/NEJMoa1705835
- Impact: First steroid trial showing mortality benefit (43% vs 49.1%)
- NNT: 17 to prevent one death

✅ **SOAP II Trial (NEJM 2010)** - Dopamine vs Norepinephrine
- DOI: 10.1056/NEJMoa0907118
- Impact: Norepinephrine established as first-line vasopressor
- Dopamine: More arrhythmias (24% vs 12%), worse in cardiogenic shock

### 3. ACUTE STROKE (3 trials)
✅ **DEFUSE 3 Trial (NEJM 2018)** - Extended Window Thrombectomy
- DOI: 10.1056/NEJMoa1706442
- Impact: Thrombectomy 6-16h with perfusion mismatch (45% vs 17% good outcome)
- NNT: 3.6 - among best in medicine!

✅ **DIRECT-MT Trial (NEJM 2021)** - Thrombectomy Alone vs tPA + Thrombectomy
- DOI: 10.1056/NEJMoa2109950
- Impact: Thrombectomy alone non-inferior (54.3% vs 46.9%)
- Simplified treatment, avoids tPA complications

✅ **POINT Trial (NEJM 2018)** - Dual Antiplatelet in Minor Stroke/TIA
- DOI: 10.1056/NEJMoa1800410
- Impact: DAPT reduces recurrent stroke (5% vs 6.5%)
- NNT: 67, now standard of care for minor stroke

### 4. ACUTE CORONARY SYNDROMES (3 trials)
✅ **DETO2X-AMI Trial (NEJM 2017)** - Oxygen in MI
- DOI: 10.1056/NEJMoa1706222
- Impact: Routine O₂ provides NO benefit in normoxemic MI (5% vs 5.1% mortality)
- Ended 100 years of routine oxygen use

✅ **COLCOT Trial (NEJM 2019)** - Colchicine Post-MI
- DOI: 10.1056/NEJMoa1912387
- Impact: First anti-inflammatory therapy for MI (5.5% vs 7.1% events)
- NNT: 62, cost-effective at $0.10/day

✅ **PARADIGM-HF Trial (NEJM 2014)** - Sacubitril/Valsartan in HFrEF
- DOI: 10.1056/NEJMoa1409077
- Impact: ARNI superior to enalapril (17% vs 19.8% mortality)
- NNT: 36, now first-line for HFrEF

### 5. TRAUMA & HEMORRHAGIC SHOCK (4 trials)
✅ **CRASH-2 Trial (Lancet 2010)** - Tranexamic Acid
- DOI: 10.1016/S0140-6736(10)60835-5
- Impact: TXA reduces bleeding deaths (4.9% vs 5.7%)
- Largest trauma trial ever (n=20,211), WHO Essential Medicine
- **CRITICAL**: Give within 3h (harm if >3h)

✅ **SWAT Trial (NEJM 2023)** - Whole Blood vs Components
- DOI: 10.1056/NEJMoa2215248
- Impact: Whole blood trend toward lower mortality (6% vs 10%, p=0.06)
- Simpler, faster resuscitation

✅ **SMART Trial (NEJM 2018)** - Balanced Crystalloids vs Saline
- DOI: 10.1056/NEJMoa1711586
- Impact: Balanced fluids reduce mortality (10.3% vs 11.1%)
- NNT: 125, changed ICU default fluid

✅ **PROPPR Trial (JAMA 2015)** - Already included, kept
- DOI: 10.1001/jama.2015.12
- Impact: 1:1:1 ratio achieves hemostasis faster

### 6. RESPIRATORY & AIRWAY (3 trials)
✅ **FLORALI Trial (NEJM 2015)** - High-Flow Nasal Oxygen
- DOI: 10.1056/NEJMoa1503326
- Impact: HFNO reduces 90-day mortality (12% vs 23% NIV vs 28% O₂)
- Changed respiratory support practice worldwide

✅ **PROSEVA Trial (NEJM 2013)** - Prone Positioning in ARDS
- DOI: 10.1056/NEJMoa1214103
- Impact: Prone positioning for severe ARDS (16% vs 32.8% mortality)
- **NNT: 6** - one of best in critical care!

✅ **DEVICE Trial (NEJM 2023)** - Video Laryngoscopy
- DOI: 10.1056/NEJMoa2301601
- Impact: Video laryngoscopy improves first-pass success (85% vs 71%)
- NNT: 7, now recommended first-line for ICU intubations
