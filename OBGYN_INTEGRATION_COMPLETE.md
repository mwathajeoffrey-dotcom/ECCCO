## 🔍 Key Clinical Content Highlights

### Cardiac Disease (30 questions)
- Valvular disease: Mitral stenosis PBMV, aortic stenosis management
- Congenital: WHO classification, tetralogy of Fallot (40-50% mortality)
- PPCM: TTN mutations (10-15%), bromocriptine 2.5mg BID
- Mechanical valves: ESC 2024 shared decision-making approach
- Endocarditis prophylaxis: NOT needed for delivery per 2024 guidelines

### Diabetes (30 questions)
- GDM screening: 1-hour GCT ≥140, 3-hour OGTT Carpenter-Coustan criteria
- Targets: Fasting <95, 1-hr pp <140, 2-hr pp <120, HbA1c <6.0% (ADA 2025)
- Insulin: Basal-bolus first-line therapy
- Delivery: A1GDM 39-40 weeks, A2GDM 39 weeks
- CGM: 2024 emphasis on continuous glucose monitoring

### Hypertensive Disorders (30 questions)
- **CHAP Trial 2022**: Treat ≥140/90, target 130-135/80-85 (WITHOUT FGR increase)
- First-line: Labetalol, nifedipine XL
- Aspirin: <16 weeks for high-risk patients
- Superimposed preeclampsia: 15-25% risk in chronic HTN
- Delivery: 38-39 weeks for uncomplicated chronic HTN

### Thromboembolism (30 questions)
- VTE risk: 15-20 per 10,000 deliveries, 5-fold increase
- LMWH: Prophylactic 40mg daily, therapeutic 1mg/kg q12h
- **APS**: Revised Sydney 2023 criteria, obstetric APS reduces loss from 70-80% to 20-30%
- **ASRA 2024**: Prophylactic LMWH hold 12h, therapeutic hold 24h for neuraxial
- Duration: 6 weeks postpartum minimum, 3 months total

### Infectious Disease (30 questions)
- **HIV U=U**: Undetectable=untransmittable (<0.1% transmission with ART)
- Hepatitis B: TDF 300mg 28-32 weeks if VL >200,000
- **CDC 2024**: Universal Hepatitis C screening
- GBS: Updated 2024 guidelines, screening 35-37 weeks
- COVID-19: Vaccination strongly recommended all trimesters

### Renal Disease (30 questions)
- CKD stages: 1-2 excellent (<10% decline), 3b-4 high risk (50-80% preterm)
- **Dialysis intensification**: ≥20 hours/week (85-90% live birth vs 40-50% conventional)
- Transplant: Wait 1-2 years, switch MMF to azathioprine ≥6 weeks pre-conception
- Preeclampsia vs flare: sFlt-1/PlGF ratio, complement levels
- AKI: Preeclampsia/HELLP most common cause (30-40%)

### Thyroid Disorders (30 questions)
- **TSH ranges (ATA 2024)**: T1 0.1-2.5, T2 0.2-3.0, T3 0.3-3.0 mIU/L
- Levothyroxine: Increase 25-30% immediately when pregnancy confirmed
- **ATD protocol**: PTU first trimester, switch to methimazole 13-16 weeks
- TRAb: Monitor 18-22 weeks if >3-5x ULN (1-5% fetal thyrotoxicosis)
- Postpartum thyroiditis: 5-10% incidence, 50% if TPO+

### Hematologic Disorders (30 questions)
- Anemia: T1/T3 <11 g/dL, T2 <10.5 g/dL (physiologic hemodilution)
- **Gestational TCP**: 70-80% of pregnancy thrombocytopenia, benign
- **ITP**: Treat <30K or <50K near delivery, IVIG/prednisone first-line
- **Neuraxial safe**: >70-80K per ASRA 2024
- Sickle cell: 50% VOC, 30-40% preterm, multidisciplinary care
- VWD: Most common inherited (1-2%), check levels 36 weeks goal >50%
- Transfusion: <7 g/dL restrictive strategy (2024 AABB/ASH)
