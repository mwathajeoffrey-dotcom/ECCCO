## ✅ KEEP OLDER TRIALS (>5 years) - NO RECENT REPLACEMENT AVAILABLE

These landmark trials remain the BEST and ONLY available evidence in their domains:

### Cardiac Arrest & Resuscitation
- **ROC ALPS Trial** (2016, 9 years old) - **KEEP** ✅
  - **Why**: Only head-to-head RCT comparing amiodarone vs lidocaine for VF
  - **Status**: Still referenced in 2020 ACLS guidelines, no newer trial exists
  - **Impact**: Established amiodarone as preferred antiarrhythmic

### Sepsis & Septic Shock
- **SOAP II Trial** (2010, 15 years old) - **KEEP** ✅
  - **Why**: Only large RCT comparing dopamine vs norepinephrine as first-line vasopressor
  - **Status**: Landmark trial that established norepinephrine as standard
  - **Impact**: Changed worldwide practice, no newer vasopressor comparison trial

### Acute Stroke  
- **DEFUSE 3 Trial** (2018, 7 years old) - **KEEP** ✅
  - **Why**: Extended thrombectomy window to 6-16 hours, paradigm-shifting trial
  - **Status**: Still current standard of care for late-window thrombectomy
  - **Impact**: Revolutionary - wake-up strokes now treatable, no newer trial

### Acute Coronary Syndromes
- **DETO2X-AMI Trial** (2017, 8 years old) - **KEEP** ✅
  - **Why**: Only large RCT on routine oxygen therapy in normoxemic MI patients
  - **Status**: Ended 100 years of routine oxygen use, changed ACS guidelines
  - **Impact**: No newer oxygen trial exists, practice-changing

- **COLCOT Trial** (2019, 6 years old) - **KEEP** ✅
  - **Why**: First trial proving anti-inflammatory therapy (colchicine) effective post-MI
  - **Status**: Emerging therapy, being adopted into practice
  - **Impact**: No newer colchicine post-MI trial, represents cutting-edge treatment

- **PARADIGM-HF Trial** (2014, 11 years old) - **KEEP** ✅
  - **Why**: Landmark trial establishing sacubitril/valsartan (Entresto) as superior to ACE-I
  - **Status**: First major advance in HF therapy in decades, now first-line per guidelines
  - **Impact**: No newer ARNI trial, this IS the pivotal trial

### Trauma & Hemorrhagic Shock
- **CRASH-2 Trial** (2010, 15 years old) - **KEEP** ✅
  - **Why**: Landmark TXA trial (n=20,211 patients), largest trauma trial ever conducted
  - **Status**: WHO Essential Medicine, TXA now standard worldwide
  - **Impact**: No newer TXA trauma trial needed - this trial is definitive

- **SMART Trial** (2018, 7 years old) - **KEEP** ✅
  - **Why**: Only large pragmatic RCT comparing balanced crystalloids vs normal saline
  - **Status**: Changed fluid resuscitation practice in ICUs/EDs worldwide
  - **Impact**: No newer crystalloid comparison trial

### Respiratory & Airway
- **FLORALI Trial** (2015, 10 years old) - **KEEP** ✅
  - **Why**: Only RCT showing high-flow nasal oxygen reduces mortality vs standard O2/NIV
  - **Status**: Established HFNO as first-line for hypoxemic respiratory failure
  - **Impact**: Critical during COVID-19 pandemic, no newer HFNO mortality trial

- **PROSEVA Trial** (2013, 12 years old) - **KEEP** ✅
  - **Why**: Only RCT proving prone positioning reduces mortality in severe ARDS
  - **Status**: NNT=6 (extraordinary), stopped early for overwhelming benefit
  - **Impact**: Standard of care for ARDS, no newer prone positioning trial needed

**Total Older Trials to Keep**: 10 landmark trials (irreplaceable evidence)
