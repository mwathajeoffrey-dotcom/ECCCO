Question 1: What is the most common cause of postpartum hemorrhage?

A. Uterine atony
B. Genital tract trauma
C. Retained placenta
D. Coagulopathy

📚 Explanation

Correct Answer: A. Uterine atony

Uterine atony is the most common cause of postpartum hemorrhage, accounting for approximately 70-80% of cases. It occurs when the uterus fails to contract adequately after delivery, preventing effective compression of the placental blood vessels.
