## 🔍 METHODOLOGY

### Systematic Review Process
For each pre-2020 trial, evaluated:
1. ✅ Has newer RCT (2020-2025) addressed same question?
2. ✅ Has recent meta-analysis (2020-2025) synthesized evidence?
3. ✅ Do 2020+ guidelines supersede the trial?
4. ✅ Is the trial still actively cited in current practice?

### Decision Criteria
**REMOVE if:**
- Newer trial provides same/better evidence
- Recent meta-analysis synthesizes the topic
- Guidelines comprehensively cover the topic
- Trial redundant with other evidence

**KEEP if:**
- Only trial answering the specific question
- No recent meta-analysis supersedes it
- Actively cited in 2020+ guidelines
- Practice-defining/landmark status
- Mega-trial too definitive to replace
