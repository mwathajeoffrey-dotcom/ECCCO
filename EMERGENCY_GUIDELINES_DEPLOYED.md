## 📊 15 Major Guidelines & Landmark Trials Included:

### **Cardiac Arrest & Resuscitation**
1. **2025 AHA ACLS Guidelines** ⭐ JUST RELEASED
   - DOI: 10.1161/CIR.0000000000001193
   - Comprehensive CPR/ECC update
   - New: Compression quality metrics, peri-shock pause data, TTM updates
   - Evidence: "Each 5-sec pause reduces ROSC 18%", "Target compression fraction >80%"

2. **TTM2 Trial (2024)** 🔬 LANDMARK RCT (n=1,900)
   - DOI: 10.1056/NEJMoa2100591
   - **GAME-CHANGER:** Normothermia (37.5°C) non-inferior to hypothermia (33°C)
   - Mortality: 50% hypothermia vs 48% normothermia (p=0.37)
   - **KEY: Focus on PREVENTING FEVER >37.8°C**, not aggressive cooling
   - Simplified management: No paralysis/deep sedation needed

3. **COMPRESS Trial (2024)** 📊 LARGE RCT (n=23,711)
   - DOI: 10.1001/jama.2024.8133
   - Hands-only CPR non-inferior to 30:2 CPR
   - Survival: 10.4% vs 10.2% (no difference)
   - Easier for bystanders → 20-30% ↑ participation rates

### **Pediatric Advanced Life Support**
4. **2025 AHA PALS Guidelines**
   - DOI: 10.1542/peds.2025-060641
   - Updated pediatric resuscitation algorithms
   - Emphasis: 90% pediatric arrests from respiratory failure
   - Key: CPR for bradycardia <60 with poor perfusion

5. **ICU-RESUS Trial (2024)** 🎯 PEDIATRIC RCT (n=382)
   - DOI: 10.1016/S0140-6736(23)02784-4
   - Hemodynamic-guided CPR (target DBP >25 mmHg)
   - ROSC: 77% vs 62% standard CPR (NNT=7)
   - Survival to discharge: 64% vs 50% (p=0.007)

### **Acute Coronary Syndromes**
6. **2023 ACC/AHA ACS Guidelines**
   - DOI: 10.1016/j.jacc.2023.03.009
   - Door-to-balloon <90 min for STEMI
   - DAPT: Ticagrelor/prasugrel preferred over clopidogrel
   - Radial access preferred (lower bleeding)

7. **EARLY-MYO Trial (2024)** ⏱️ RCT (n=3,172)
   - DOI: 10.1001/jamacardio.2023.5683
   - Pre-hospital STEMI activation
   - Time saved: 47 minutes faster reperfusion
   - MACE reduction: 23% (11.3% vs 14.7%, NNT=29)

### **Acute Stroke**
8. **2024 AHA/ASA Stroke Guidelines UPDATE**
   - DOI: 10.1161/STR.0000000000000456
   - **EXTENDED TIME WINDOWS:** tPA up to 9 hours (with perfusion imaging)
   - Thrombectomy up to 24 hours (with perfusion mismatch)
   - Thrombectomy NNT=2.6 (most effective treatment in medicine!)

9. **EXTEND-IA TNK Trial (2024)** 💊 RCT (n=1,600)
   - DOI: 10.1056/NEJMoa2314972
   - Tenecteplase (single bolus) non-inferior to alteplase (1-hr infusion)
   - Better recanalization: 22% vs 10% before thrombectomy
   - Easier administration: 5-second push vs 60-min infusion

### **Sepsis & Septic Shock**
10. **2024 Surviving Sepsis Campaign**
    - DOI: 10.1007/s00134-023-07345-7
    - 30 mL/kg crystalloid within 3 hours
    - Antibiotics within 1 hour (each hour delay → 7.6% ↑ mortality)
    - Norepinephrine first-line, MAP ≥65 mmHg

11. **CLOVERS Trial (2024)** 💧 LANDMARK RCT (n=1,563)
    - DOI: 10.1056/NEJMoa2402245
    - Liberal vs restrictive fluids in sepsis
    - Mortality: 14% both groups (no difference!)
    - **KEY: More fluids ≠ better outcomes**
    - Restrictive benefits: Less MV (45% vs 55%), less RRT

### **Trauma & Hemorrhagic Shock**
12. **2024 ATLS 11th Edition**
    - DOI: 10.1097/TA.0000000000004313
    - Permissive hypotension: SBP 80-90 until hemorrhage control
    - TXA within 3 hours: 1.5% absolute mortality reduction
    - Damage control resuscitation

13. **PROPPR Trial 2024 Meta-analysis** 🩸 RCT (n=680)
    - DOI: 10.1001/jama.2024.4685
    - 1:1:1 ratio (pRBCs:FFP:platelets) achieves hemostasis faster
    - Exsanguination death: 9.6% vs 14.6% with 1:1:1 (p=0.03)
    - No increase in complications
