## 🎯 Content Coverage

### Topic 1: Placenta Previa (pp-001 to pp-030)
- Complete vs partial vs marginal classification
- Transvaginal ultrasound diagnosis
- Risk factors: prior cesarean (strongest), multiparity
- Management: pelvic rest, corticosteroids, delivery at 36-37 weeks
- Placenta accreta spectrum risk (50-67% with prior cesarean + previa)
- Double setup procedure (historical context)
- Complications and emergency management

### Topic 2: Placental Abruption (pa-001 to pa-030)
- Clinical presentation: painful bleeding, uterine rigidity
- DIC management: fibrinogen <200 mg/dL predicts massive transfusion
- Concealed vs revealed abruption (20% concealed)
- Couvelaire uterus pathophysiology
- Trauma protocols and seat belt sign
- Tranexamic acid (within 3 hours)
- Massive transfusion protocols (1:1:1 ratio)
- Acute cortical necrosis, chronic abruption
- Emergency delivery timing and indications

### Topic 3: Preeclampsia (pre-001 to pre-030)
- Diagnostic criteria: BP ≥140/90 + proteinuria or severe features
- Severe features: BP ≥160/110, thrombocytopenia, elevated LFTs, renal insufficiency
- Magnesium sulfate protocols: loading 4-6g IV, maintenance 1-2g/hr
- Magnesium toxicity management: calcium gluconate 1g IV
- Delivery timing: 37 weeks (no severe features), <34 weeks (severe features after steroids)
- HELLP syndrome: Hemolysis, Elevated Liver enzymes, Low Platelets
- Eclampsia management: protect airway, magnesium, stabilize then deliver
- Antihypertensive therapy: labetalol, hydralazine, nifedipine
- Postpartum monitoring: 24 hours magnesium, BP check within 7-10 days
- PRES (Posterior Reversible Encephalopathy Syndrome)
- Long-term cardiovascular disease risk counseling

### Topic 4: Preterm Labour (ptl-001 to ptl-030)
- Tocolytic agents: nifedipine (first-line), indomethacin (≤32 weeks), magnesium
- Antenatal corticosteroids: betamethasone 24-34 weeks
- Magnesium sulfate for fetal neuroprotection <32 weeks
- GBS prophylaxis in preterm labor <37 weeks
- Cervical cerclage: history-indicated, ultrasound-indicated, rescue cerclage
- Fetal fibronectin testing: negative predictive value >99%
- Short cervix management: <25mm, vaginal progesterone
- PPROM management: antibiotics (ampicillin + erythromycin → amoxicillin + azithromycin)
- Chorioamnionitis: immediate delivery with broad-spectrum antibiotics
- Late preterm corticosteroids (34-36 weeks)
- Tocolysis contraindications and duration (48 hours maximum)

### Topic 5: Obstetric Emergencies (obe-001 to obe-030)
- Umbilical cord prolapse: elevate presenting part, emergency cesarean
- Shoulder dystocia: HELPERR mnemonic (McRoberts + suprapubic pressure)
- Postpartum hemorrhage: definition (≥500 mL vaginal, ≥1000 mL cesarean)
- PPH medication sequence: oxytocin → methergine/misoprostol → hemabate
- Intrauterine balloon tamponade: 300-500 mL fill
- Uterine inversion: immediate manual replacement before cervical ring contracts
- Amniotic fluid embolism: sudden hypoxia, cardiovascular collapse, DIC
- Massive transfusion: 1:1:1 ratio (RBC:FFP:Platelets)
- Tranexamic acid: within 3 hours, 1g IV
- VBAC/TOLAC: uterine rupture risk factors and management
- Fourth-degree laceration repair sequence
- Shock index in obstetrics: HR/SBP, >0.9 concerning
- Traumatic uterine rupture after MVA
- Tubo-ovarian abscess management
- Fetal resuscitation: Apgar scoring, NRP guidelines

### Topic 6: Gynecologic Pain & Bleeding (gpb-001 to gpb-030)
- Postmenopausal bleeding: endometrial biopsy essential
- Endometrial thickness: <4mm has 96-99% NPV for cancer
- Endometrial cancer: 75-80% endometrioid adenocarcinoma (Type I)
- Lynch syndrome: 40-60% lifetime endometrial cancer risk
- Ovarian torsion: enlarged ovary, absent Doppler flow, urgent laparoscopy
- Pelvic inflammatory disease: ceftriaxone + doxycycline
- Fitz-Hugh-Curtis syndrome: perihepatitis from PID
- Complex atypical hyperplasia: 25-40% concurrent cancer risk
- Tamoxifen effects: endometrial thickening, 2-3x cancer risk
- Endometriosis: progressive dysmenorrhea, uterosacral nodularity
- Uterine fibroids: intramural most common (70%), management options
- Adnexal mass evaluation: CA-125, ultrasound features, gynecologic oncology referral
- GnRH agonists: mechanism of action, indications, side effects
- Lichen sclerosus: clobetasol treatment, 4-5% malignancy risk
- Endometrial cancer staging: FIGO system
- Surgical treatment: total hysterectomy with BSO

### Topic 7: Vasa Previa & Uterine Rupture (vr-001 to vr-030)
- Vasa previa definition: fetal vessels crossing internal os
- Classic triad: membrane rupture, painless bleeding, fetal bradycardia
- Delivery: scheduled cesarean at 34-37 weeks
- Diagnosis: transvaginal ultrasound with color Doppler
- Velamentous cord insertion: vessels in membranes, unprotected by Wharton's jelly
- Perinatal mortality: 56-95% undiagnosed, <3% diagnosed prenatally
- Risk factors: IVF, multiple gestation, bilobed placenta, low-lying placenta
- Apt test: differentiates fetal from maternal blood
- Uterine rupture risk: classical scar 4-9%, low transverse 0.5-1%
- TOLAC/VBAC: candidate selection, success factors
- Prostaglandin contraindication in TOLAC
- Myomectomy with cavity entry: high rupture risk
- Rupture presentation: sudden pain, Category III tracing, maternal shock
- Uterine dehiscence vs rupture: asymptomatic vs symptomatic
- Interpregnancy interval: <18 months increases rupture risk
- Two prior cesareans: 1.8-3.7% rupture risk
- Double-layer uterine closure: lower rupture risk
- Epidural in TOLAC: safe and recommended
- Emergency management and maternal mortality prevention
