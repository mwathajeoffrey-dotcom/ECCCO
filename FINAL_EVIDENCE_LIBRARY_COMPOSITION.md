## ✅ JUSTIFICATION FOR KEEPING OLDER TRIALS

### ROC ALPS (2016) - 9 years old ✅
- Only head-to-head RCT comparing amiodarone vs lidocaine
- Cited in 2020 ACLS Guidelines as primary evidence
- No newer antiarrhythmic trial exists

### SOAP II (2010) - 15 years old ✅
- Only large RCT (n=1,679) comparing dopamine vs norepinephrine
- Definitive trial establishing norepinephrine as first-line
- Cited in 2021 Surviving Sepsis Guidelines
- No vasopressor comparison trial has superseded it

### DEFUSE 3 (2018) - 7 years old ✅
- Paradigm-shifting extended thrombectomy window trial
- Still current standard of care for late-window thrombectomy
- No newer extended-window trial exists

### DETO2X-AMI (2017) - 8 years old ✅
- Only large oxygen therapy RCT in normoxemic MI patients
- Ended 100 years of routine oxygen practice
- Cited in 2021 ACC/AHA Chest Pain Guidelines
- No newer oxygen trial exists

### COLCOT (2019) - 6 years old ✅
- Only post-MI colchicine trial
- Emerging therapy, being adopted 2020-2025
- No newer colchicine post-MI trial exists

### PARADIGM-HF (2014) - 11 years old ✅
- THE definitive ARNI trial (sacubitril/valsartan)
- First major advance in HF therapy in decades
- Entresto now first-line based on this trial
- No newer ARNI trial exists (this IS the pivotal trial)

### CRASH-2 (2010) - 15 years old ✅
- Mega-trial (n=20,211) - largest trauma trial ever
- WHO Essential Medicine based on this trial
- Cited in 2024 ATLS 11th Edition
- Too definitive to be replaced

### SMART (2018) - 7 years old ✅
- Largest crystalloid comparison (n=15,802)
- Despite later conflicting trials (BaSICS, PLUS), remains most cited
- Practice-changing trial
- No clear replacement despite later studies

### FLORALI (2015) - 10 years old ✅
- Only HFNO trial showing mortality benefit
- COVID-19 studies different pathophysiology
- No newer general HFNO mortality trial exists

### PROSEVA (2013) - 12 years old ✅
- NNT=6 (extraordinary!)
- Only prone positioning RCT showing such dramatic benefit
- Standard of care validated during COVID-19
- Irreplaceable evidence
