## 🎯 Topic Categories

### Obstetric Emergencies (180 questions)

#### High-Risk Pregnancy Conditions
- **Placenta Previa** (`placenta-previa.ts`)
  - Placental implantation over cervical os
  - Classification, diagnosis, management
  - Delivery timing and recommendations
  
- **Placental Abruption** (`placental-abruption.ts`)
  - Premature placental separation
  - Risk factors, clinical presentation
  - Emergency management protocols

#### Hypertensive Disorders
- **Preeclampsia & Eclampsia** (`preeclampsia.ts`)
  - Diagnostic criteria and severe features
  - HELLP syndrome
  - Magnesium sulfate protocols
  - Antihypertensive management
  - Delivery timing and postpartum care

#### Preterm Complications
- **Preterm Labour & PPROM** (`preterm-labour.ts`)
  - Tocolytic therapy
  - Antenatal corticosteroids
  - Magnesium for neuroprotection
  - GBS prophylaxis
  - PPROM management

#### Acute Emergencies
- **Obstetric Emergencies** (`obstetric-emergencies.ts`)

  - Shoulder dystocia (HELPERR)
  - Postpartum hemorrhage
  - Uterine inversion

- **Vasa Previa & Uterine Rupture** (`vasa-previa-rupture.ts`)

  - Uterine rupture risk factors
  - TOLAC/VBAC considerations

### Medical Comorbidities in Pregnancy (240 questions - 2024-2025 Guidelines)

#### Cardiovascular Disease
- **Cardiac Disease in Pregnancy** (`cardiac-disease-pregnancy.ts`)
  - Valvular disease (mitral stenosis, aortic stenosis)
  - Congenital heart disease (WHO classification, tetralogy of Fallot, Marfan syndrome)
  - Peripartum cardiomyopathy (PPCM)
  - Mechanical valve anticoagulation
  - **Guidelines:** ESC 2023/2024, ACC/AHA 2024

#### Endocrine Disorders
- **Diabetes in Pregnancy** (`diabetes-pregnancy.ts`)
  - GDM screening (1-hour GCT, 3-hour OGTT)
  - Glucose targets and HbA1c goals (<6.0% per ADA 2025)
  - Insulin and oral agent management
  - Delivery timing and macrosomia management
  - **Guidelines:** ADA 2025 Standards of Care, Endocrine Society 2024

- **Thyroid Disorders in Pregnancy** (`thyroid-disorders-pregnancy.ts`)
  - Hypothyroidism and levothyroxine adjustment
  - Hyperthyroidism and antithyroid drugs (PTU vs methimazole)
  - TSH pregnancy-specific ranges (T1: 0.1-2.5, T2: 0.2-3.0, T3: 0.3-3.0)
  - TRAb monitoring and fetal thyrotoxicosis
  - Postpartum thyroiditis
  - **Guidelines:** ATA 2024, Endocrine Society 2024

#### Hypertensive Disorders
- **Hypertensive Disorders in Pregnancy** (`hypertensive-disorders.ts`)
  - Chronic hypertension (CHAP trial 2022 evidence: treat ≥140/90, target 130-135/80-85)
  - Superimposed preeclampsia
  - Medication management (labetalol, nifedipine)
  - Aspirin prophylaxis (<16 weeks)
  - **Guidelines:** CHAP trial 2022, AHA/ACC 2024, ACOG 2024, ISSHP 2024

#### Hematologic Disorders
- **Thromboembolism in Pregnancy** (`thromboembolism-pregnancy.ts`)
  - VTE prevention and treatment (15-20 per 10,000 deliveries, 5-fold risk increase)
  - LMWH dosing (prophylactic 40mg daily, therapeutic 1mg/kg q12h)
  - Antiphospholipid syndrome (revised Sydney 2023 criteria)
  - Neuraxial timing (prophylactic LMWH hold 12 hours, therapeutic hold 24 hours per ASRA 2024)
  - **Guidelines:** ASH 2024, ACCP 2021, ASRA 2024

- **Hematologic Disorders in Pregnancy** (`hematologic-disorders-pregnancy.ts`)
  - Anemia (IDA, B12, folate - T1/T3 <11, T2 <10.5 g/dL)
  - Thrombocytopenia (gestational 70-80%, ITP treatment thresholds <30K or <50K near delivery)
  - Bleeding disorders (von Willebrand disease 1-2%, hemophilia carriers)
  - Sickle cell disease (50% VOC, 30-40% preterm, multidisciplinary care)
  - Transfusion medicine (restrictive <7 g/dL)
  - **Guidelines:** ASH 2024, ACOG 2024, NHLBI 2024

#### Infectious Disease
- **Infectious Disease in Pregnancy** (`infectious-disease-pregnancy.ts`)
  - HIV (U=U concept: undetectable=untransmittable <0.1% transmission)
  - Hepatitis B (TDF 28-32 weeks if VL >200,000) and C (5-6% vertical transmission)
  - Herpes simplex virus (suppressive acyclovir ≥36 weeks)
  - Group B Streptococcus (screening 35-37 weeks, CDC 2024 updated guidelines)
  - TORCH infections, COVID-19, vaccination schedules (Tdap every pregnancy 27-36 weeks)
  - **Guidelines:** CDC 2024, NIH 2024, WHO 2024

#### Renal Disease
- **Renal Disease in Pregnancy** (`renal-disease-pregnancy.ts`)
  - CKD staging and pregnancy outcomes (stage 1-2 excellent <10% decline, stage 3b-4 high risk)
  - Dialysis intensification (≥20 hours/week improves live birth from 40-50% to 85-90%)
  - Renal transplant management (wait 1-2 years, switch MMF to azathioprine ≥6 weeks pre-conception)
  - Preeclampsia vs CKD flare differentiation (sFlt-1/PlGF ratio, complement levels)
  - **Guidelines:** KDIGO 2024, ASN 2024

## 📚 Question Structure

Each question follows this standardized format:

```typescript
{
  id: string,              // e.g., "pp-001", "pre-015"
  question: string,        // Clinical scenario or question
  options: string[],       // 4 answer options
  correctIndex: number,    // Index of correct answer (0-3)
  explanation: string,     // Detailed explanation with clinical reasoning
  references: string[],    // Evidence-based sources (ACOG, RCOG, etc.)
  difficulty: string,      // 'easy' | 'medium' | 'hard'
  topicId: string,         // Topic identifier for filtering
  category: string         // 'obstetric' | 'gynecologic'
}
```

## 🔄 Adding New Topics

When adding new OB/GYN topics:

1. **Create new file** in this folder (e.g., `gestational-diabetes.ts`)
2. **Follow naming convention**: `kebab-case.ts`
3. **Export questions array**: `export const gestationalDiabetesQuestions: Question[] = [...]`
4. **Update `index.ts`**: Add import and export
5. **Update this README**: Add to table and categories
6. **Update API**: Add topic to `/api/topics/route.ts`
