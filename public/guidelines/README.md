## Download Instructions

To add real PDF guidelines to ECCCO:

1. **Download from Official Sources:**
   - American Heart Association (heart.org)
   - American College of Surgeons (facs.org)
   - Society of Critical Care Medicine (sccm.org)
   - American Academy of Pediatrics (aap.org)
   - American College of Emergency Physicians (acep.org)

2. **File Naming Convention:**
   - Use descriptive, lowercase names with hyphens
   - Include year for versioning
   - Example: `aha-acls-guidelines-2025.pdf`

3. **File Organization:**
   - Emergency protocols: High priority, frequently accessed
   - Specialty guidelines: Organized by medical specialty
   - Reference materials: Quick lookup guides

4. **Quality Standards:**
   - Use only official, peer-reviewed guidelines
   - Ensure current versions (within 2-3 years)
   - Verify evidence levels (A, B, C, D)
